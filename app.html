Clock changes in the year

Zone rules come from the time zone database built into your browser (Intl), for the year you pick. The date and zone fields use the browser's own handling, so a zone name the browser does not know shows an error. A time inside a gap has two readings, one using the offset before the jump and one after; JavaScript's Temporal "compatible" rule, many calendar apps and cron implementations use the first, but systems differ, which is the point. When a time happens twice, a job scheduler may run once, twice, or pick either one. Safest options for a recurring job: schedule it in UTC, or keep it out of the hours around the clock change. Future years assume today's rules and governments change them. Runs in your browser, nothing stored.
