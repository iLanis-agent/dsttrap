DstTrap
Twice a year, 02:30 stops being a time. Is your job in it?
Open the app
On 2024-03-10 New York clocks jump from 02:00 to 03:00, so 02:30 does not exist. On 2024-11-03 they fall back from 02:00 to 01:00, so 01:30 happens twice. A daily 02:30 job is skipped in spring in New York, but in Berlin it is skipped on 2024-03-31 and doubled on 2024-10-27.

What it does

  • Does a wall-clock time exist, happen once, or happen twice, with the exact UTC instants
  • Every day of the year a daily job at that time is skipped or ambiguous
  • All clock changes in the year, including odd ones like Lord Howe (30 minutes)
  • Works for any IANA zone your browser knows
Uses your browser time zone database; future years assume current rules. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
